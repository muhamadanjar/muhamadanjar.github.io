# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective clients, employers, and technical collaborators reviewing Muhamad Anjar's experience and selected digital work.

## Product Purpose

Present a trustworthy, navigable portfolio that makes it easy to assess capabilities, browse work, and start a conversation.

## Brand Personality

Professional, technical, and calm. The experience should feel precise and considered rather than flashy.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS gradients, over-rounded cards, decorative visual noise, and portfolio-template clichés.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the work and the person behind it.
- Make technical depth easy to scan.
- Use restraint to communicate confidence.
- Keep every path to contact clear.
- Respect the reader's time and device.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast where practical, retain clear focus states, provide descriptive image alternatives, and honor reduced-motion preferences.
